About

Smart-Search Floor Manager reads class timetables and works out which rooms are free right now, and for how long. Nothing about availability is hardcoded.

Engine: getRoomAvailability, calculateContinuousAvailability, filterRooms, findAvailableRooms, rankRooms and parseUserQuery are shared by the AI search and the filters.

Your data: edit the TIMETABLES and ROOMS constants in index.html. Campus hours are 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
